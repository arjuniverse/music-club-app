# 🎵 Dhwani Club

**Dhwani Club** is a modern digital platform designed to bring together music lovers, artists, performers, and creative students within a college community. The application provides a centralized space for students to discover upcoming music and arts events, participate in competitions, showcase their talents, join workshops, and connect with fellow artists.

The platform aims to make college music and cultural activities more engaging, organized, and accessible while encouraging students to explore and express their creativity.

## 💡 Project Concept

The **Dhwani Club** platform acts as a digital hub for all music and creative activities within the college. Students can discover events, register for activities, showcase their talents, and stay connected with the club.

The application helps club coordinators manage events and participants efficiently while giving students a platform to express their creativity, discover new opportunities, and connect with fellow artists and performers.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Clone the Repository


